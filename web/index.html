DREHLSS / FIELD MANUAL

Your first delivery.

The minimap gets you there. You still need to get out, take the box and ring the bell.

  1. Park. Get out. Take the box.

    Drive with WASD. Stop near the orange delivery marker. Press V to exit, then E beside your van to collect the selected parcel. “In your hands” confirms you have it.

  2. Ring the actual recipient.

    Walk up to the marked entrance and press R. Click the bell beside your recipient’s name. Press Esc to close the intercom. You must ring before leaving or throwing the parcel.

  3. Make your questionable delivery.

    E places the box at a nearby doorstep. T throws it ahead: it bounces off walls and lands. If a resident catches you, finish the timed dialogue or press E beside them to hand it over.

  4. Provide photographic evidence.

    After placing the box, or once a thrown box has stopped, press F. Face the box from under 9 metres, centre it in the frame, then press Space. The next unfinished delivery is selected automatically after the photo is saved. A handover needs no photo.

Another day, same neighbourhood. At 17:00, or once all couriers finish, compare your scores and vote for tomorrow’s neighbourhood: the current area or two random places. Solo uses the same rules. You have 20 seconds; ties stay here. Your van gets fresh parcels and your career totals stay. M opens your current route map without ending the shift. To move somewhere else, use Settings → Change neighbourhood.

On time? 0 points. A proper delivery earns 0 KPI. A wrong-address photo earns 100; getting caught reduces it to 25. Timed intercom and name challenges can add bonuses.

Dogs mean trouble. NPC dogs growl for 2.8 seconds before jumping. Leave their range or break their line of sight. A bite makes you yelp, jump and freeze briefly: 1.2 seconds for NPC dogs, 1.5 for player dogs. Your parcel and KPI stay with you. NPC dogs recover for 8 seconds after a jump.

Cursor: Esc releases it; click the game to look around again. Tab / Shift+Tab browse deliveries. Q beside the van returns the held box so you can take another. F1 opens settings. The shared shift clock keeps running while this guide is open.
