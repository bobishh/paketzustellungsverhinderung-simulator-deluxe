# DREHLSS field manual

## Your first delivery

1. Drive with **WASD** to the orange delivery marker. Stop and press **V** to leave the van.
2. Press **E** beside your van to collect the selected parcel. Check that the HUD says **In your hands**. Driving to the address does not take the box out automatically.
3. Walk up to the marked entrance and press **R**. Click the bell beside your recipient's name. Press **Esc** to close the intercom. Ringing is required before placing or throwing the parcel.
4. Press **E** at a doorstep to put the box down, or **T** to throw it ahead. A thrown parcel collides with walls and lands; wait until it stops. You can pick a dropped box up again with **E** nearby.
5. Press **F** to open the camera. Face the box from under 9 metres with a clear line of sight. Centre it in the frame and press **Space**. A saved photo finishes the delivery and automatically selects the next unfinished stop.

If a recipient catches you, finish the timed name dialogue or press **E** beside them to hand over the box. Handovers need no photograph.

## Why zero points?

This is a bad-courier simulator. A proper delivery earns **0 KPI**. Photographing a wrong-address delivery earns **100 KPI**, or **25** if you were caught. The timed all-bells challenge, name dialogue and rare broken-intercom opportunity offer separate bonuses.

## Where is the cursor?

**Esc** releases the mouse. Click the game to capture it again. **Tab / Shift+Tab** inspect the next/previous delivery; browser and system combinations such as Cmd+Tab are left alone. Tab retains normal focus navigation in forms and dialogs.

You can inspect another address while holding a box, but that does not change the parcel in your hands. Press **Q** beside your van to return it, then **E** to collect the selected parcel.

**F1** opens browser settings. **How to play** is available from the header and settings. The shared 09:00–17:00 shift clock continues while menus are open.

## Dog bites

NPC dogs growl for **2.8 seconds** before jumping. Leave their five-metre range or break their line of sight. After a jump they recover for **8 seconds**.

A bite makes the courier jump once, flail their arms and briefly lose control, with a small camera jolt and a browser yelp. The HUD shows the remaining shock time: **1.2 seconds** for an NPC dog, **1.5 seconds** for a player's dog. The parcel and KPI are preserved. Sound effects can be disabled in settings.

As a dog: **E** bites a nearby courier (20 KPI) or scratches a shut door; **Space** jumps, **B** barks and **G** marks another player's van. Biting a resident stops them for three seconds.

## Neighbourhood map and the next day

**M** (or the HUD’s Map button) opens a map of your current streets, van and numbered delivery stops. Zoom or fit the route, click a numbered stop to inspect it, then press M or Esc to return. This keeps the current shift and parcel. **Settings → Change neighbourhood** is the separate action for leaving the shift and choosing a new place.

Players in one mesh share the same addresses and completed deliveries, including when joining late. The host confirms the first completion for each address that day. Delivery KPI and career credit go to that courier. The numbered colours in the player list match their direction markers around the minimap; the larger orange diamond points to your selected delivery.

Solo and multiplayer use the same end-of-day rules: 17:00, or all active couriers completing their routes. The score table then opens a 20-second vote: the current neighbourhood and two randomly proposed cities. Each player gets one vote and may change it until the decision. A unique majority wins; ties or no votes keep the current area. The host commits the result to the mesh. Day 2, Day 3 and subsequent days keep the connection and career totals, refill parcels, reset residents and close doors.

Neighbourhood cars stop for obstacles, and pedestrians walk along street edges and try to go around obstructions. Block a car with your parked van for at least four seconds while a parcel is being delivered to earn **25 KPI**. Blocking a pedestrian earns **5 KPI**. Each NPC awards this bonus at most once per day. Parking without an active parcel earns nothing.
